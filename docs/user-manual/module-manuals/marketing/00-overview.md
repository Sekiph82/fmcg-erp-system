# Marketing Module — Overview

**Route:** `/dashboard/marketing`  
**Permission required:** `marketing.view`

The Marketing module manages the full commercial marketing stack — campaign planning and ROI tracking, promotions, trade program management (TPM), digital advertising, social media, customer segmentation, influencer partnerships, e-commerce, brand spend, field visits, and market intelligence.

## Tab Summary

| Tab | Key | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Overview | `overview` | Dashboard: campaign KPIs, ROI, budget vs actual, status chart |
| Campaigns | `campaigns` | Campaign list and lifecycle management |
| Promotions | `promotions` | Trade and consumer promotion records |
| Schemes | `promotions-schemes` | Promotion scheme configurations |
| TPM | `tpm` | Trade Promotion Management — structured trade deals |
| Trade Spend | `trade-spend` | Trade spend tracking by type and region |
| Ads | `ads` | Digital advertising — Meta, Google, TikTok, YouTube |
| Social Media | `social-media` | Social media content, posts, and sentiment |
| Segments | `segments` | Customer segmentation and relationship tracking |
| Influencers | `influencers` | Influencer registry and content assignments |
| E-Commerce | `ecommerce` | Online store integrations and performance |
| Brand Spend | `brand-spend` | Above-the-line and below-the-line brand spend |
| Visits | `visits` | Field sales and trade audit visits |
| Market Intel | `market-intel` | Competitor and market intelligence |
| Analytics | `analytics` | Cross-channel marketing analytics |
